A usage-aligned model

Easy entry. Commercial demand. Shared growth.

The franchisee operates the local business and controls customer pricing. EVODS charges a franchise fee per kWh, so the model scales with actual energy delivered.

1

EVODS funds the capital equipment

Qualified sites avoid the conventional upfront burden of purchasing the charging infrastructure.

2

You secure commercial customers

Build recurring relationships with fleets that require dependable daily charging and operational support.

3

You set the customer sale price

The franchisee retains the spread between the cost of electricity and the price charged to fleet clients.

4

EVODS earns per kWh

The franchise fee is usage based, aligning EVODS support with franchisee growth and energy throughput.

It is the commercial-EV equivalent of owning a gas station—built for vehicles that need a fill-up every day.
